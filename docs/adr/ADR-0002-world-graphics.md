# ADR-0002: World merges the graphics, weather and brand mods; their game settings are edits on the base game

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01
- Amended: 2026-10-31 (1.4.1: wet roads no longer a mirror; decisions 4, 6 and 8)

## Context

- The maintainer ran about a dozen Workshop and local mods for the world around the road: Juninho944's Weather_3.9,
  Asphalt_improved, grass_4.0_lite and tree_improved_4k; DIOX's Beautiful Nature and Beautiful Water; Darkcaptain's
  Realistic Rain; Grimes' Realistic Rain Reflections (not on the Workshop); Kass's Realistic Rain & Water & Thunder
  Sounds; DBM Road Signs & Markings 4K; Real companies & gas stations (MLH82, 4of25); and Actual Day-/Nighttime
  (1061306287) for the date.
- They collide on 389 paths. Several ship whole copies of 1.61 game files made for older versions: grass_4.0's
  `terrain_material*.sii` lack 11 of 1.61's terrain materials, Beautiful Nature's `game_data.sii` carries economy
  settings, Rain Reflections ships stale truck headlights and a stale junction prefab, Real companies names trailer
  looks 1.61 no longer has.
- Weather_3.9 made almost all rain heavy, very foggy and dark at night and at dawn/dusk, so headlights reached little.
- ETS2's date never advances; vanilla fixes it at 21 June (sun at 22:00), and 1061306287's weekly real-date sync
  brings dark 10:00 mornings back every winter.

## Decision

1. **Same model as ADR-0001.** Third-party asset mods are `sources.yaml` entries; game settings they change are
   carried as `base_edits` on the current base game, generated by a tool with `--check`; World's own files are in
   `overrides/`. Local build only; every author is credited in the description.
2. **Load order (low -> high, higher wins):** grass_4.0 < Beautiful Nature < Asphalt_improved < DBM < tree_improved <
   Weather_3.9 < Realistic Rain < Kass < Real companies. DIOX sits below Juninho944's set on purpose. Every collision
   is declared by `overrides:`, resolved in `resolutions.yaml` (gas price stand to Real companies, rain streaks and
   puddle normal maps to `overrides/`), or excluded at the losing source.
3. **Game settings as edits** (`world/graphics.yaml`, `world/brands.yaml`): grass density and 225 terrain tints,
   Beautiful Nature's leaves LOD only, Realistic Rain's windshield drops, Real companies' 256 company names and 113
   trailer paint-job masks. A `trailer_look` change is carried only when that look exists (`plain` or a
   `company_paint_job/<look>.sii`); company files 1.61 never includes are dropped.
4. **Weather_3.9's climate is the mod's design** and ships whole (only `default` and `underground`); World edits it
   (`tools/night_climate.py`, `world/climate.yaml`):
   - Rain mix: variants grouped by sky into light/medium/heavy/storm; rain amount and wetness clamped into the
     class; lightning only in the storm class; integer `weight`s for shares day 40/35/18/7, night 45/35/15/5.
     Day wetness: heavy 0.55-0.70, storm 0.70-0.80 (were 0.60-0.85 / 0.80-0.95 to 1.4.0, a near-mirror road).
     `thunderstorm_probability` 1.0 so the weights alone set the storm share.
   - Night (sun <= -4 deg): rain fog <= 0.0035 from >= 30 m, wetness <= 0.6, rain streak light >= 30.
   - Dawn/dusk (-4 to +10 deg): rain fog <= 0.004 from >= 20 m.
   - The tool refuses to write a decimal where the game file has an integer: a decimal `weight` made the whole
     `bad.sii` fail to load and rain crash the game (dev.2).
5. **Date** (`world/environment.yaml`): fixed day 270 (27 September), `summer_time` 1. About 12 h of daylight
   everywhere; light ~07:00 and dark ~19:00 in central Germany/Czechia (the map has one clock, so the west runs
   later and the east earlier). Replaces 1061306287. No seasons.
6. **Realistic Rain Reflections is a tool input, not a source** (`tools/rain_reflections.py`). Its wheel-track
   puddle normal maps are blended (Reoriented Normal Mapping, BC5, at RRR's 2048 px with a full mip chain) into
   the 49 winning road normal maps, so Asphalt_improved keeps its surface detail. The ruts are strengthened where
   Asphalt's detail is rough (universal 1.6x, blkw 1.4x, country templates 1.0x), so the tracks reach RRR's own
   depth. Its reflection/shine values are carried onto 263 base materials; the 58 junction materials 1.61 gives
   no normal map get the blended map of the road template with the same colour texture (plus `.tsnmapcalc`), so
   no RRR file ships unblended. Its colour textures, junction models, headlights, climate and rain files are not
   used. The blended textures are git-ignored and regenerated by the tool.
7. **Kass:** lightning (20 bolt/cloud sets), thunder bank, and the water textures 1.61 uses (wave maps, river, muddy
   and green water, panorama, Balkan water plants), replacing Beautiful Water; beaches stay grass_4.0's. Its rain
   sounds, particles, spray, windshield, `game_data` and camera are not used.
8. **Rain look:** Realistic Rain's rain and windshield drops, its streak brightness raised to vanilla's 1.0 (0.6
   made them vanish at night); the game's own wheel spray (Realistic Rain's and Kass's tried and rejected).
   `rain_planar_strength` and `rain_cube_strength` 0.5 (base 0.9 mirrored the sky evenly across the whole road,
   a lake); `rain_specular_strength` stays 0.9 for the glare along the wheel tracks. Wet roads need the game's
   rain reflections and reflection quality set high; at their lowest every version looks dry.
9. **One archive.** A 6.68 GiB / 10,501-entry HashFS archive (2.7 GB of data and every folder listing past 4 GiB)
   loads clean and no slower than 5 parts. `max_part_bytes` is 32 GiB because the planner estimates Workshop
   sources unpacked (~2.4x).
10. **Not merged:** Sound Fixes Pack (its value is the author's per-version rework of ~480 definition files) and
    Better Flares (City Lights ships ~780 whole street-lamp models that cannot be edits); both stay separate mods.

## Consequences

- A **game update**: extract the new base, set `base_game.version`, rerun every tool, rebuild. Tools and `megapack
  check` fail loudly on units, files or looks that moved, instead of shipping stale copies.
- A **source update** (Steam re-sync): `megapack updates` flags it; re-extract, `megapack lock` (changed collision
  inputs in `resolutions.yaml` go stale and need review), rerun the tools, rebuild. Accepted for 10 sources.
- After a clone, `python tools/rain_reflections.py` must run before a build (the blended textures are not in git).
- The rain mix, fog limits, puddle strength and date are plain constants in the tools and `world/environment.yaml`,
  tuned by play tests.
- World is never redistributed, which also respects the authors' "do not re-upload" terms.
