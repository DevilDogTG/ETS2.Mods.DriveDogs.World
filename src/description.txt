[normal]The world around the road for ETS2 1.61: weather, rain, roads, grass and trees from Juninho944, DIOX and Realistic Rain in one pack, with wet-road puddles, lightning, a realistic mix of light and heavy rain, a fixed equinox date for natural day and night, and a fix for poor headlight reach in night rain; realistic train lengths and Cip's real train and level-crossing sounds. Game settings are built on the current game files, so what the game adds in updates keeps working.
[red]Enable every part. Place ABOVE Sound Fixes Pack (optional) and any other train sound or train length mod.
[red]Do not load these, they are built in: Weather_3.9, Asphalt_improved, grass_4.0_lite, tree_improved_4k (Juninho944), Beautiful Water, Beautiful Nature (DIOX), Realistic Rain, Realistic Rain Reflections, Realistic Rain & Water & Thunder Sounds (Kass), Realistic Train Lengths, Real Train Sounds ETS2.
[red]Also disable any day/night or date mod (e.g. Workshop 1061306287): World sets the date.

[green]Changelogs

[orange]v0.2.0
[normal]- Graphics: Weather_3.9, Asphalt_improved 1.5, grass_4.0_lite, tree_improved_4k (Juninho944), Beautiful Water and Beautiful Nature (DIOX), Realistic Rain; where they overlap, Juninho944's win over DIOX, and Realistic Rain's rain wins over Weather's
- Night rain: less fog and road wetness at night in rain and drizzle, so headlights reach further (Weather_3.9's night rain was far foggier than the game's)
- Wet-road reflections back to the game's strength
- Their game settings are carried onto the 1.61 files instead of old full copies: keeps the 11 terrain materials 1.61 added, drops Beautiful Nature's economy and unused sound settings
- Wheel spray in rain: the game's own
- Wet roads: Realistic Rain Reflections' puddles in the wheel tracks, blended into Asphalt_improved's road surfaces; wet reflections on sidewalks, tiles, concrete, parking lots and junctions
- Lightning: Kass's lightning bolts and flashes (20 kinds)
- Rain mix: mostly light and medium rain, heavy rain less often, thunderstorms rare (Weather_3.9 made nearly all rain heavy)
- Day and night: a fixed equinox date (23 September), about 12 hours of daylight everywhere; no sun at 22:00, no dark mornings

[orange]v0.1.0
[normal]- Realistic Train Lengths rebuilt on the 1.61 game files: fixes the missing Dutch Flirt (traffic.frt.nl) error and keeps the new NL, BE and LU trains
- Real Train Sounds (Cip): train engine, wheel and horn sounds and per-country level-crossing sounds
- Cip's sounds for the DV12 and E.405 locomotives now reach the trains (1.61 renamed their files)
