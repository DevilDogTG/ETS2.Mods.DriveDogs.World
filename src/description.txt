[normal]The world around the road for ETS2 1.61: weather, rain, roads, grass and trees from Juninho944, DIOX and Realistic Rain in one pack, with a fix for poor headlight reach in night rain; realistic train lengths and Cip's real train and level-crossing sounds. Game settings are built on the current game files, so what the game adds in updates keeps working.
[red]Enable every part. Place ABOVE Sound Fixes Pack (optional) and any other train sound or train length mod.
[red]Do not load these, they are built in: Weather_3.9, Asphalt_improved, grass_4.0_lite, tree_improved_4k (Juninho944), Beautiful Water, Beautiful Nature (DIOX), Realistic Rain, Realistic Train Lengths, Real Train Sounds ETS2.
