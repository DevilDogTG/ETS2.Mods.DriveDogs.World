[normal]The world around the road for ETS2 1.62: weather, rain, roads, grass, trees and water from Juninho944, DIOX, Realistic Rain and Kass, DBM's trees and grass, terrain, road surfaces, guard rails, rocks and walls, water and 4K road signs and markings, country traffic-light lenses, and real company and gas station brands in one pack, with wet-road puddles, lightning and thunder, a realistic mix of light and heavy rain, a fixed late-September date for natural day and night, and a fix for poor headlight reach in night rain; people waiting at bus stops; realistic train lengths and Cip's real train and level-crossing sounds. Game settings are built on the current game files, so what the game adds in updates keeps working.
[red]Place ABOVE Sound Fixes Pack (optional) and any other train sound or train length mod.
[red]Do not load these, they are built in (disable them, including Workshop subscriptions): Weather_3.9, Asphalt_improved, grass_4.0_lite, tree_improved_4k (Juninho944), Beautiful Water, Beautiful Nature (DIOX), Realistic Rain, Realistic Rain Reflections, Realistic Rain & Water & Thunder Sounds (Kass), DBM Road Signs & Markings 4K, DBM Vegetation, DBM Terrain Textures, DBM Guard Rails 4K, DBM World & Buildings HD, DBM Water, DBM Road Textures, Real companies & gas stations, Busy Bus Stations, Realistic Train Lengths, Real Train Sounds ETS2, Different lenses of traffic lights.
[red]Also disable any day/night or date mod (e.g. Workshop 1061306287): World sets the date.
[red]Remove the DriveDogs: Better Flares Traffic Lights add-on: World now does everything it did (with or without Better Flares).

[orange]v1.5.2
[normal]- Rain at dawn and dusk is no longer a dark, short view: the eye adapts to the dim rainy light as it does in the game without weather mods, so the road ahead and the headlight beam show again (around sunrise and sunset, about 4 degrees of sun below the horizon to 10 above). Night and daytime rain are unchanged
- Takes DBM Road Signs & Markings 4K's re-upload of 2.3: three sign images (two Spanish junction diagram signs for Puertollano and a Russian curve arrow) are padded to a size the game compresses cleanly; the signs look as before

[orange]v1.5.1

[orange]v1.5.0
[normal]- DBM's world graphics are built in (disable these mods): DBM Vegetation (3D leaves and grass, scanned bark), DBM Terrain Textures (scanned ground, dirt and parking-lot textures), DBM Guard Rails 4K, DBM World & Buildings HD (rocks, walls, fences, barriers) and DBM Water (lake waves, foam, shores and beaches); where they overlap, DBM's win over Juninho944's and DIOX's, and DBM's lake waves over Kass's (Kass keeps the rivers and distant water)

- People at bus stops follow the bus stops 1.62 moved
- Real companies' gas-station signs get the price boards and looks 1.62 added (Primel and Pegi), keeping their real brands
- One archive, now about 12 GB (DBM's HD textures)

[orange]v1.4.2

[orange]v1.4.1
[normal]- Wet roads in rain look like wet asphalt again, not a mirror or a lake: the sky reflection is softer so the dark road shows through, and the glare along the wheel tracks stays
- Deeper wheel tracks from Realistic Rain Reflections on the common road surfaces, at its full texture size
- Junctions (crossings, roundabouts, slip-road joins) get the same wheel tracks as the road next to them
- Heavy rain and thunderstorms leave the road a bit less wet
- Wet roads need the game's rain reflections and reflection quality set high (Graphics settings); at their lowest the road looks dry

[orange]v1.0.0

- Rain at night and at dawn/dusk: less fog and road wetness at night, less fog around 07:00 and 19:00, so headlights reach further; falling rain stays visible at night
- Day and night: a fixed date just after the equinox (27 September): in central Europe light from about 07:00 and dark from about 19:00; no sun at 22:00, no dark mornings
- Wheel spray in rain: the game's own
- The mods' game settings are carried onto the 1.61 files instead of old full copies: keeps the 11 terrain materials 1.61 added, drops Beautiful Nature's economy and unused sound settings
- One archive (no parts)
